How to Play

  1. Get three in a row. Horizontally, vertically, or diagonally on the 3×3 grid wins the game.
  2. Take turns. Player X always moves first, then players alternate one square at a time.
  3. Create or join a room. Creating gives you a 5-letter code — send it to a friend. Joining just needs that code.
  4. Stay in sync. The server tracks the real board, so refreshing your browser won't lose your seat in the match.
  5. No board, no win? If all 9 squares fill with no winner, it's a draw — the scoreboard tracks those too.
  6. Practice solo. Play vs Computer for a local match against an AI opponent at three difficulty levels.
